# HealthConnect – Data Analytics

## Project Overview

HealthConnect is a healthcare analytics project focused on understanding patient appointment attendance and no-show behaviour.

The project uses data analytics to identify patterns that can support better appointment management, patient engagement and informed decision-making.

## Project Journey

The HealthConnect Data Analytics work progressed through four key stages:

- **Week 5 – Initial Analysis & Dashboard Development**
- **Week 6 – Advanced Analytics & Decision Support**
- **Week 7 – Testing, Validation & Refinement**
- **Week 8 – Final Integration & Decision Support**

## Week 5 – Initial Analysis

The project began with data preparation, exploratory analysis, KPI development and dashboard creation.

### Work Completed
- Data preparation and validation
- Exploratory data analysis
- KPI development
- No-show pattern analysis
- Power BI dashboard development
- Initial business recommendations

## Week 6 – Advanced Analytics

The analysis was extended to investigate important factors associated with appointment no-shows.

### Areas Analysed
- Booking lead time
- Previous no-show history
- Distance to clinic
- Reminder status
- Appointment type

An Advanced Analytics & Decision Support page was added to the Power BI dashboard.

## Week 7 – Testing & Validation

The existing dashboard and analytical findings were systematically tested.

### Validation Completed
- KPI validation
- Analytical finding validation
- Dashboard slicer and interaction testing
- Appointment outcome reconciliation
- Dashboard usability review

The tested KPIs and major analytical findings remained consistent, with no major dashboard errors identified.

Data Science model-testing results were also reviewed as a complementary cross-track input.

## Week 8 – Final Integration & Decision Support

The final stage consolidated the validated analysis into decision-support insights and practical recommendations.

### Final KPIs

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Appointments | 5,000 |
| Attendance Rate | 46.28% |
| No-Show Rate | 48.46% |
| Cancellation Rate | 5.26% |
| Reminder Coverage | 72.68% |

### Key Findings

- Longer booking lead times were associated with higher observed no-show rates.
- Previous no-show history was associated with higher current no-show rates.
- Greater distance from the clinic was associated with higher observed no-show rates.
- Appointments without reminders had a higher observed no-show rate.
- Appointment type showed relatively similar no-show rates across categories.

These findings represent observed associations and do not establish causation.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Prioritise follow-up for patients with previous no-show history.
- Strengthen reminder and appointment confirmation strategies.
- Provide additional confirmation for appointments booked far in advance.
- Investigate distance-related barriers to attendance.
- Continue monitoring appointment no-show patterns.
